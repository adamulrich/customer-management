Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Prime Pianos uses customer information in connection with the Pitch Ledger customer management application for piano tuning services.

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Pitch Ledger and Prime Pianos collect, use, store, and share personal information in connection with the customer management application used for piano tuning services.

This application is used to manage customer records, appointments, invoices, reminders, follow-up messages, and occasional promotional communications.

Information We Collect

We may collect and store the following information:

  • Customer name
  • Mailing address
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Appointment history
  • Appointment notes
  • Pricing and tax information related to services provided
  • Communication preferences, including reminder and marketing settings
  • Records of invoices, reminders, follow-up messages, and marketing messages sent through the platform

We may also collect limited technical information required to operate the application securely, such as account login information for authorized business users.

How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Schedule and manage piano tuning appointments
  • Send invoices and payment-related information
  • Send appointment reminders
  • Send follow-up messages after service
  • Maintain business and tax records
  • Send promotional messages or special offers where permitted
  • Improve and maintain the customer management system

Communications

If enabled by the business, customers may receive:

  • Transactional emails
  • Transactional text messages
  • Appointment reminders
  • Follow-up service messages
  • Promotional or marketing emails

Customers can request that marketing communications stop at any time. Operational communications related to active or past services may still be sent when reasonably necessary.

For text messaging, message frequency may vary depending on appointments, reminders, follow-up needs, and customer communication preferences. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Terms

By providing a mobile phone number and consenting to receive text messages, customers agree to receive service-related text messages from Prime Pianos through the Pitch Ledger platform. These messages may include appointment reminders, follow-up messages, invoice-related messages, and other service communications.

Message frequency varies by customer activity and preferences.

Message and data rates may apply.

Customers may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, where supported, or by contacting the business directly.

For help with SMS messages, customers may reply HELP, where supported, or contact the business directly.

Mobile phone numbers will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Payment Information

This application may include links to third-party payment services such as Venmo. Payment transactions are processed by those third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. This application does not store full payment card information.

Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate the application and send communications. These may include:

  • Back4App / Parse for application hosting, authentication, and database services
  • Resend for email delivery
  • Twilio for SMS delivery
  • GitHub Pages for static site hosting

These providers may process personal information on our behalf solely to provide their services to us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share personal information only:

  • With service providers that help us operate the application
  • When necessary to send requested or business-related communications
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or lawful requests
  • To protect the rights, safety, and property of the business or others

Data Retention

We retain customer and appointment information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide services
  • Maintain customer history
  • Keep accounting and tax records
  • Resolve disputes
  • Comply with legal obligations

Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or anonymized when practical.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

Customers may request to:

  • Review or correct their contact information
  • Update communication preferences
  • Request deletion of certain information, subject to legal or business recordkeeping obligations
  • Opt out of marketing emails or texts

To make a request, customers should contact the business directly using the contact information below.

Customers may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, where supported, or by contacting the business directly.

Children’s Privacy

This application is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy
